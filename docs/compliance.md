# Evidence checks, with sources

Sources checked 27 October 2026. These are record checks for a private lender or mortgage fund lending to businesses in New Zealand or Australia. They are not legal advice, not a compliance certificate and not a substitute for your own AML/CTF programme, trust deed or lending policy.

| Code | Source | What the record check looks for |
|---|---|---|
| AML-CDD | Australia: [Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2006A00169/latest/text), section 6 (making a loan in the course of a lending business is a designated service) and section 32 (identify the customer before providing it). New Zealand: [AML/CFT Act 2009](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2009/0035/latest/), section 5 (lending is a financial institution activity) and section 14 (standard customer due diligence). | A borrower on an approved or live loan, or an investor holding an allocation, with no identity check date. `settle` and `allocate` refuse until one is recorded. |
| AML-REVIEW | Australia: section 36 of the 2006 Act (ongoing customer due diligence). The two-year interval is a local default, not a statutory period. | A borrower on a live loan whose identity check is older than two years. |
| BUSINESS-PURPOSE | Australia: [National Credit Code](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00134/latest/text) (Schedule 1 to the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009), section 5(1)(b) and the business purpose declaration presumption in section 13. New Zealand: [Credit Contracts and Consumer Finance Act 2003](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2003/0052/latest/), section 11 (credit used primarily for personal, domestic or household purposes is consumer credit). | A loan with no declaration date, or one dated after settlement. `settle` refuses without it. A declaration does not protect a lender who knew the money was for personal use. |
| WHOLESALE-CERT | Australia: [Corporations Act 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00818/latest/text), sections 708(8)(c) and 761G(7)(c): a qualified accountant's certificate given no more than two years before the offer. New Zealand: the wholesale investor exclusions in Schedule 1 of the [Financial Markets Conduct Act 2013](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2013/0069/latest/). The two-year interval is applied to New Zealand certificates as a local default. | A wholesale or sophisticated investor with no certificate date, or one older than two years. `allocate` refuses. |
| RETAIL-INVESTOR | Offers to retail investors need a registered scheme and disclosure, which this desk does not hold. | A retail investor holding an allocation. `allocate` refuses new retail allocations. |
| RG45-B5-INDEPENDENT | [ASIC Regulatory Guide 45](https://download.asic.gov.au/media/qpuljitz/rg45-published-05-march-2026.pdf) (5 March 2026), Benchmark 5, RG 45.47(b): the valuer is independent. | A mortgage security whose valuer is not recorded as independent. |
| RG45-B5-BEFORE-ISSUE | RG 45.47(e)(i): an independent valuation before the loan is issued and on renewal. | A live loan with a mortgage security valued after settlement or never. `settle` refuses; `extend` needs a valuation from the last 90 days (a local default). |
| RG45-B5-AS-IF-COMPLETE | RG 45.47(e)(i)(A): development property valued on both an "as is" and an "as if complete" basis. | A construction loan security with no as if complete value. |
| RG45-B6-LVR | RG 45.53(b) and (c): no more than 70% of the latest "as if complete" valuation for property development, and no more than 80% of the latest market valuation otherwise. | A loan whose limit is above the benchmark against its security. Each loan also carries its own `max_lvr_bps` from your lending policy; `settle` and `drawdown` refuse above it. |
| RG45-B6-STAGED | RG 45.53(a): development funds are provided in stages based on independent evidence of progress. | A construction draw with no progress report recorded. `drawdown` refuses a construction draw without `--evidence`. |

RG 45 sets benchmarks for registered mortgage schemes offered to retail investors in Australia. Many wholesale funds and New Zealand lenders adopt the same thresholds in their lending policy, which is why the checks use them as defaults. Change them in a migration through `/customise` if your policy differs.

Other local defaults: arrears count from the day after an interest or fee charge is posted; valuations older than 12 months and insurance expiring within 30 days go on `attention`; interest not charged for 35 days goes on `attention`.

Interest is charged on an actual/365 basis. Each day uses the balance at the end of the day before. Interest-only loans charge interest on principal; capitalised loans charge it on the full balance. The default rate applies after maturity and from a recorded default date. Check this against your loan agreements before you rely on it.

No identity numbers, bank account numbers or dates of birth are stored. The importer refuses a loan tape that carries those columns. Record where the identity evidence is held, not the evidence itself.
